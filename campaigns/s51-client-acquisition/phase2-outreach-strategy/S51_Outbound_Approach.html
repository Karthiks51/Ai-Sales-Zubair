02

Two lenses, not one: ICP fit and services fit

The question raised was fair. ICP fit answers who they are. It does not answer whether they need what we sell. A lead must clear both gates to enter the sequence.

ICP only → Watchlist / nurture
Neither → Exclude
Lens 1 · WHO

ICP fit

Are they the right kind of company and buyer?

  • B2B SaaS $5–30M ARR, or Data & AI services $10–500M
  • US / UK / Western Europe served market
  • Marketing leader or founder with budget authority
  • Not Series C+, not B2C, not a nonprofit, not domestic-only
Lens 2 · WHAT

Services fit

Do they have a problem S51 sells into, right now, with budget?

  • A live pain we solve: positioning, narrative, brand, content, website
  • A trigger showing the need is active now (rebrand, raise, new CMO, launch)
  • Budget for the mapped offer, $6K/mo to $80K project
  • Maps to a named S51 pillar and offer, not a vague "could use marketing"

The plan

One stale list of 88 realistically yields 1–2 clients. Reaching 20 needs roughly 8–12 lists of this quality, or one properly-sourced current list of ~400–600 in-ICP contacts. So we run these 14 as a proof batch, lock the pain-theme and opener that pull replies, then scale sourcing against the exact ICP and services-fit profile these winners share.
