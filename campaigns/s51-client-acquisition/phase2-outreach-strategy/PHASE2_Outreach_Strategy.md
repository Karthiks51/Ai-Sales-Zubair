## The strategic reality, stated plainly

Phase 1 established that the source list is a ~2017-2018 export: **80% of the 88 people have changed jobs and 77% of the CSV emails are dead.** That changes the shape of this campaign in three ways:

1. **We are not writing to the CSV company. We are writing to where the person works now.** Every sequence below targets the lead's *current* employer and role, graded fresh against S51's ICP. Deborah Holstein isn't a Hightail CMO anymore; Jim Yang isn't at Illuminate Education. The value of the list is that it's a *network of proven B2B-SaaS marketing leaders and founders*, most of whom moved to new companies. We chase the person, not the stale row.

2. **The addressable set from this list is ~14-18 people, not 88.** Only 4 leads are a Strong ICP fit and 10 more are Moderate. The other 74 are Weak or Excluded because their current company is out of S51's sweet spot (too big, B2C, a nonprofit, a VC fund, India/APAC-domestic) or can't be verified. This document builds real sequences for the 14, plus a watchlist of 6 early-stage founders worth nurturing.

3. **The 20-client goal cannot come from this list alone.** Honest math is below. Hitting 20 signed clients requires topping up the pipeline with fresh ICP sourcing (Apollo/LinkedIn), because 14-18 qualified conversations will not convert to 20 clients at any realistic close rate.

### Sequencing rules across the batch (from the S51 playbook)

- **Stagger, never batch.** Never touch two leads at the same company on the same day; there are no same-company clusters in this 14, so simply stagger Tier A starts by 1-2 days each.
- **LinkedIn warm-up before Email 1:** Day -3 profile view, Day -2 connection request (the no-pitch note in each block), Day 0 Email 1. For the Highly/Moderately active leads (Cohen, Bhashyam), add an optional Day -5 genuine comment on a live post.
- **Cadence:** Email 1 Day 0, Email 2 Day 3, Email 3 Day 7, Email 4 Day 12, Email 5 Day 18. A/B the two subject lines on the first send of each tier and keep the winner.
- **Contact-first for dead-email leads:** for any lead flagged "Likely dead," find the current-company address (pattern-match the company's format, or use LinkedIn/Apollo) before Day 0. Do not send to the CSV address; it will bounce and hurt domain reputation.

## Tracking & prioritization (how to run this)

- **One row per lead in a tracker** (CRM or sheet) with: tier, current company, ICP fit, channel, the single pain theme, opener pattern, current-email-status (found / pending / bounced), and the 5 send dates. A ready-to-import CSV ships alongside this doc as `S51_Phase2_Sequence_Tracker.csv`.
- **Score and re-rank weekly** on reply signal. A Tier B lead who replies jumps the queue; a Tier A lead who bounces and can't be re-contacted drops to the watchlist.
- **Stop rules:** stop the sequence on any reply (positive or negative); stop after Email 5 with no reply and move to a 90-day nurture; stop immediately if the person says no.
- **Measure the messaging, not just the leads.** With only 14 in the proof batch, the goal is to learn which pain theme and opener pattern earn replies, then carry the winner into the scaled sourcing round. Track reply-by-pain-theme and reply-by-opener-pattern.
- **Two subject lines per email are already written (A/B).** They test different angles, not reworded versions. Keep the winner per tier.
